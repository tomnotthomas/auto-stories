Step 1 · Say it

Pick + “What’s the story?” + tone

Picked photos fill the top as an auto-sizing grid (up to 10, no scroller) with an Add tile. Below: the story field and one optional tone chip, then “Create my story.” No stepper.

Step 2 · Payoff

The finished story (cinematic)

Ordered, captioned, full-bleed — photo is the hero. Tap the photo to move through frames; a coach mark points at the caption — you tap the text itself to edit (no button). One action: Refine story. Per spec, Phase 1 ends here and nothing leaves the app — the hand-off is Phase 2.

Open full size ↗

Inside Refine — edit the caption

From the payoff, “Edit text” (or “Refine story”) opens this: move the caption anywhere on the photo, drag a corner to resize, rewrite the words, adjust size, regenerate, toggle the legibility background.

Refine · Text

Place & edit the caption

The caption is edited directly in the frame (cursor in the text) — no duplicate field. Selection box + corner handles resize it; the “drag to move” tag repositions it. The sheet holds only the extras: text size, Regenerate, and the legibility-background toggle. Done returns to the story.

Waiting & error states

The generate call can take a moment and has a hard timeout, so the wait is engaging and every failure shows a specific message (spec: fair-use cap, resilient generation).

Generating

Engaging wait, not a spinner

The user's own photos cycle behind while the steps check off in sequence — read photos → find the story order → write captions. Visible progress on the AI doing the valuable part, not a frozen checkmark.

Error · Capacity

At capacity

Free-tier daily cap / rate limit hit. An animated sloth (sways, lazy wave, blinks) + clear-then-funny copy ("we're maxed out, take it slow, try again in a few minutes") — softens the failure while saying exactly what happened.

Error · Timeout

Took too long

The hard timeout fired so it never hangs. An animated dog (tail wags, blinks) + copy that says what happened ("didn't come back in time, even the dog got bored — your photos are safe"). Try again, or back to the picker.

Open full size ↗
Resilient · Dropped

One photo didn't fit

A flagged photo is dropped and the story is still built from the rest (≥3). Not an error — a dismissible top banner in the headroom, kept clear of the caption, on the finished story.
